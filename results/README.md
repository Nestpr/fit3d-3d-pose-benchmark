# Results

Per-sequence metrics and tables behind every number of the paper. All files are plain CSV (and LaTeX for the tables). The 3D predictions are **not** included (they are regenerated by notebooks 01 to 04).

The folder structure is the one the notebooks use under `STORAGE_ROOT`, so the content of this folder can be copied there. With it, **Table 4 and Figs. 4 and 5 of notebook 05 (Section 3) can be reproduced without running any model**.

`<2D source>` is `poses2d_gt` (ground-truth projection), `pose2d_yolo` (YOLO-Pose) or `pose2d_rtmpose` (RTMPose).

## Columns

- **`results_per_video_*`**: one row per sequence (224 = 8 subjects × 28 exercises). `MPJPE_mm` and `PA_MPJPE_mm` are the mean errors over frames and joints, in millimetres; `frames` is the number of evaluated frames.
- **`results_per_joint_*`**: PA-MPJPE (mm) per sequence and joint, on the 17-joint skeleton of the lifting models (or the 15 joints of MVGFormer).
- **`smoothing_COMMON15_per_video.csv`**: for each model (TCPFormer, PoseMamba-L, MVGFormer) and version (original, One Euro, Savitzky–Golay, Median) and sequence: `PA_MPJPE_COMMON15_mm` and `MPJVE_mm_frame` on the COMMON15 skeleton. `smoothing_COMMON15_global.csv` has the means over the 224 sequences that appear in Tables 6 and 7.
- **`COMMON15_PA_MPJPE_per_video.csv` / `_per_joint.csv`**: COMMON15 PA-MPJPE of the three models, as used in Table 5 and Figs. 6 and 8.

## Reproducing Table 4 and Figs. 4 and 5 from these files

1. Copy `Fit3D_TCPFormer/` and `Fit3D_PoseMamba/` to your `STORAGE_ROOT` (Google Drive in Colab, `./data` locally).
2. Open `notebooks/05_evaluation_and_figures.ipynb` and run the cells up to Section 3 (Table 4 and Figs. 4 and 5).

The following sections of the notebook need the 3D predictions, so they require running notebooks 01 to 04 first.

## Notes

- The MVGFormer files come from the original evaluation run of the multi-view inference, before the notebooks were reorganized for this repository (the column `mode` still shows the name used then), with the absolute paths of the original machine removed. Their mean PA-MPJPE over the 224 sequences is 66.698 mm.
- **MVGFormer, original predictions.** Tables 5 and 6 here list 66.698 mm for the original MVGFormer, computed with the evaluation of notebooks 04 and 05. The arXiv version of the paper lists 66.691 mm for the same predictions, computed with a Procrustes variant that also applies the reflection correction to the scale; the difference is below 0.02 mm per subject and does not affect any conclusion. Every other value agrees with the paper to its printed precision.
- Notebook 05 checks every table against the paper values within explicit tolerances and writes `verification_summary.csv` (not included here, since it is regenerated by the notebook).
